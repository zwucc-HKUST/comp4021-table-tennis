THE CLUB PLAYBOOK

Find your rhythm.

Live matches keep running while this guide is open.

A D
Move sideways. Shape your return. Line up with the incoming ball. Moving left or right as you meet it changes the return angle.
W S
Move forward or back. Push forward with W for a faster return; pull back with S for a slower one. Position early, then make a small adjustment as the ball arrives.
Space
Serve when it’s your turn. Position your paddle, then press Space or select Serve. After a point, wait for the serving indicator before starting again.
C
Toggle cheat mode. Your returns glow gold and change direction when they bounce on your opponent’s half. Toggle off to stop further deflections and remove the glow.

Timing is the game.

Let the ball bounce once on your half, then meet it with your paddle. Contact makes the return automatically; there is no separate swing button. Aim to return over the net onto your opponent’s half.

Mouse or touch works, too.

Press and hold the directional buttons to move. Select Serve to start a point, Cheat to toggle the effect, and Return guide to show or hide the landing and contact aid.

Read the return guide.

Compare the blue marks for the ball’s current depth with the green line for your racket. The ring predicts the first bounce. The cross predicts where the ball reaches your racket, and the brackets mark the contact zone. A cheat deflection changes the prediction after the bounce.

Five points. Three minutes.

A miss, double bounce, double touch, or invalid return loses the point. First to 5 wins. At the 3-minute limit, the higher score wins; equal scores are a draw.
